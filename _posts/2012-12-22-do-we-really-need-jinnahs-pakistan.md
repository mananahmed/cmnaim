I wish this [essay](http://www.dailytimes.com.pk/default.asp?page=20121222story_22-12-2012_pg3_6) by Hussain Nadim had appeared in the Urdu press, for the debate he mentions rages most fiercely and incessantly in the columns and letters published in Pakistani Urdu newspapers. The author makes an urgent plea to think anew. It has been made by others too. Even I, a non-Pakistani, [once wrote](http://www.columbia.edu/itc/mealac/pritchett/00litlinks/naim/ambiguities/13iqbaljinnah.html): \[The Pakistanis\] will have to delve deep into themselves as they are now, and not as they think they were in the past, recent or remote. After all Iqbal told them:

> <span style="font-family:Book Antiqua;">"Why should I ask the 'wise men' what my beginning was?</span><span style="font-family:Book Antiqua;">I am busy discovering what my destiny is." </span>

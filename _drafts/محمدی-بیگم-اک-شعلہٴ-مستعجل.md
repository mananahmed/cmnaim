Muhammadi Begum, the co-founder with her husband, Syed Mumtaz Ali, of *Tahzīb-e Niswāñ*, was the first woman to actually edit an Urdu journal. She was also a prolific writer and organizer. Sadly, her active life lasted only ten years (1898–1908). The original English essay forms a chapter in my *A Most Noble Life* (Orient Blackswan,2022). The brilliant Urdu translation was kindly done by Dr. Arjumand Ara of Delhi, and appeared in *Imroz* (Aligarh),#8 (2018), pp. 57—88, and *Aaj* (Karachi), #104 (2018), pp. 247—284. The correct year of birth for Muhammadi Begum is 1877.

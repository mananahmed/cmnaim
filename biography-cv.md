<h1>Biography & CV</h1>

<h2>Curriculum Vitae</h2>

<p>Born June 3, 1936 in Bara Banki (India); naturalized American citizen.</p>
<p>Education: M.A. (Urdu), Lucknow University, Lucknow, 1955.<br>
M.A. (Linguistics), University of California, Berkeley, 1961.</p>

<p>Consultant, Hindi-Urdu Program, University of Minnesota, 1961-62.<br>
Lecturer, Department of Linguistics, University of Chicago, 1962-63.<br>
Instructor, Department of Linguistics, University of Chicago, 1963-68.<br>
Visiting Lecturer, University of Pennsylvania, Summer 1964.<br>
Assistant Professor, Department of South Asian Languages and Civilizations, University of Chicago, 1968-71.<br>
Visiting Lecturer, University of California, Berkeley, Summer 1968.<br>
Visiting Assistant Professor, University of Rochester, Summer 1970.<br>
Visiting Associate Professor, Aligarh Muslim University, Aligarh (India), 1971-72.<br>
Associate Professor, Department of South Asian Languages and Civilizations, University of Chicago, 1971-98.<br>
Chairman, Department of South Asian Languages and Civilizations, University of Chicago, 1985-91.<br>
Professor, Department of South Asian Languages and Civilizations, University of Chicago, 1998-2001.</p>
